Goal

Implement the paired task-render and tool-style plans: use Claude Code-style verb phrases and U+2026 ellipsis for operation batches, allow selected batches to reveal individual rows, and show the shared TaskListV2 renderer above the Spinner via Ctrl+T.

Implemented flow

  1. Changed semantic operation batches to verb-tense labels such as Read 2 files, Searching for 1 pattern, and Ran 2 bash commands; replaced the midline ellipsis with ….
  2. Made a selected grouped operation render its individual operation rows while preserving its one-line collapsed form.
  3. Added None / Tasks / Teammates expanded-view state. Ctrl+T uses two states without teammates and three states when teammates exist.
  4. Reused the task-store adapter to refresh TaskListV2 snapshots, rendered those lines above Spinner, and auto-collapsed a wholly terminal list after five seconds.
